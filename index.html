Our mission

Built on faith. Built by us.

Worship Haven is an app studio that creates its products entirely in-house. One small, dedicated team owns every step: the first idea, the design, the code, the App Store launch and the marketing that helps each app find the people it's made for.

Owning the whole journey lets us move quickly, listen closely to our users, and keep every app thoughtful, safe and true to Scripture.
