Questions

When does the next word unlock?

A new Daily Word unlocks every day at midnight, your phone's local time. Everyone playing in the same language gets the same word.

How do the colours work?

Blue means the right letter in the right spot. Gold means the letter is in the word but in another spot. Grey means the letter isn't in the word. Tap the circled "i" in the game to see this again.

How does my streak work?

Your streak grows each day you solve the Daily Word. Missing a day, or not solving the word, starts it again. Practice rounds don't affect your streak.

Why was my word not accepted?

Guesses must be real five-letter words in the language you're playing. Accents don't matter: type the letters without them.

How do I change the language?

Tap the language button at the top right of the Today screen and choose English, Français or Español. Each language keeps its own progress and streak.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Tap the bell on the Today screen. You can choose a reminder when the new word is ready, and an evening reminder if your streak is at risk. If reminders don't arrive, check Settings → Notifications → Bibole on your iPhone.

Is Bibole free?

Yes. Bibole is free to play and supported by ads. On the last try of the Daily Word, and in Practice, you can watch a short video to reveal a letter.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Bibole has no account: your streak, stats and saved verses are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Which Bible translations are used?

The World English Bible (English), the Louis Segond 1910 (French) and the Reina-Valera 1909 (Spanish). All three are in the public domain.
