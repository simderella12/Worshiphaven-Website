Bibole privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026

In short

Bibole has no account and we don't run any servers for it. Your game progress stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is what our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects to show ads, and any email you choose to send us.

Who we are

Bibole is made by Worship Haven, Paris, France ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, write to hello@myvirtualpastor.com.

What stays on your phone

Your guesses, streak, statistics, saved verses, language, reminder choices and settings are stored only on your device. We can't see them, and they're deleted when you delete the app.

Reminders are scheduled by your iPhone itself. No reminder data is sent to us or anyone else.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bibole is free because it shows ads, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

• Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when your iPhone asks
• Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
• Device information, such as model, operating system and language
• How you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crashes

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Bibole asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. If you don't consent, or you decline Apple's tracking request, you'll see ads that aren't based on your activity. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

Google handles this data under its own policy: how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Ads in Bibole are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

When you contact us

If you email us, including through "What could we improve?" in the app, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply and to improve Bibole, and we delete them when they're no longer needed.

Ratings you give through Apple's review prompt are handled by Apple, not by us.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. Bibole has no sign-in, no chat and no analytics of our own.

Children

Bibole is suitable for all ages but isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to its use, by writing to hello@myvirtualpastor.com. Since your game data never leaves your phone, deleting the app deletes it. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in France, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
